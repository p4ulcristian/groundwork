# Nothing goes in that the owner does not understand

**Decision:** an idea goes into Groundwork or a `ground-*` library only once its owner understands it: what it is, why it is there, and how it works, from the bottom. What is built and not yet understood is held back or made smaller until it is. Explaining it comes first, with small examples that run and their real output.

**Why:** Groundwork is the foundation to build on for years, mostly with code an AI writes. Its owner decides what it is; code the owner can't follow is code nobody judges. Something simpler that is understood beats something better that isn't.

**The price:** some good ideas wait, or arrive smaller, while they are learned. Building can be slower than writing.

**Instead of:** taking a construct because it is well known or because the AI recommends it, and learning it later, if ever.
