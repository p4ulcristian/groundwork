# Groundwork is frozen

> Lifted in part by [The freeze lifts for the access language](2026-10-17-the-freeze-lifts-for-the-access-language.md) and [A namespace names its stylesheets](2026-10-17-a-namespace-names-its-stylesheets.md).

**Decision:** Groundwork stays as it is. Nothing new is built: no new permission shapes, no more work on bells, no changes to styles. Nothing is deleted. A fix to something that is broken is still allowed.

**Why:** Groundwork grew in four days faster than anything used it. Its one real user so far needed a new shape or a new decision for almost every pattern it has, and that work kept piling up hard decisions. Stopping the growth first leaves room to decide calmly what Groundwork should be.

**The price:** an app that needs something Groundwork doesn't have waits, or solves it itself for now.

**Instead of:**
- going on deciding shapes one by one as the app asks for them
- cutting parts now, before deciding which parts stay
