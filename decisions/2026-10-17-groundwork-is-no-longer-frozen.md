# Groundwork is no longer frozen

> Replaces [Groundwork is frozen](2026-10-15-groundwork-is-frozen.md) and [The freeze lifts for the access language](2026-10-17-the-freeze-lifts-for-the-access-language.md).

**Decision:** work on Groundwork goes on: building, changing and deleting, one decision at a time, each written here when it is made.

**Why:** the freeze made room to decide calmly what Groundwork should be. That is decided now: Groundwork divides the business from everything else, who may see what is said in its access language, and styles follow the method an app already uses. The parts left to build follow from those decisions.

**The price:** Groundwork changes under the apps that use it again; each change keeps them working, or changes them in the same step.

**Instead of:** lifting the freeze one part at a time.
