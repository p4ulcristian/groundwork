# doorman comes from its own repository

> Changes in part [One tree; libraries.edn says, and a test checks, who uses whom](2026-10-22-one-tree-libraries-edn-checks-the-edges.md), [Every namespace says its side](2026-10-22-every-namespace-says-its-side.md), [Nine libraries, by the questions an app asks](2026-10-22-nine-libraries-by-the-questions-an-app-asks.md), [A library says where it runs; sessions and access stay apart](2026-10-22-a-library-says-where-it-runs.md) and [Groundwork's own tables are table maps, in their own folder](2026-10-22-groundworks-tables-are-maps-in-their-own-folder.md): sessions and access are no longer folders in the tree, they are one library, not two, and their tables are in its folder, not Groundwork's.

**Decision:** sessions (`groundwork.server.sessions`) and access (`groundwork.server.access`) are together one library of their own named doorman: the public repository github.com/p4ulcristian/doorman, MIT. Groundwork takes it from git by tag and sha in `deps.edn` (`io.github.p4ulcristian/doorman`, v0.1.0); malli and test.check come with it, and Groundwork no longer names them. Its one public namespace is `doorman.api`, both halves in it, every public name kept (they had none in common); its tests and its own decisions (sessions v1, access pieces 1 to 5, and "Sessions live in Postgres", "The session is the only truth", "Every scoped table names its scope", "A record may wait for its scope", "A record can belong to a visitor", "Shared rows may belong to nobody", "One request may name several scopes") live in its repository.

- **It knows nothing of Groundwork:** its tables are `doorman.sessions`, `doorman.scopes`, `doorman.roles`, `doorman.role_actions`, `doorman.memberships` (`groundwork.…` before); who is asking is the setting `doorman.asking`, read by `doorman.find_asking_person()` and `doorman.find_scopes_holding(kind, action)`; the context's keys are `:doorman/person-id` and `:doorman/transaction`. main puts its tables with the app's and makes them `[:silent …]` for sync, since doorman knows nothing of sync; `migrate-app!` grants them to the app's own role through cellar, so the folder doorman, and the functions the gates call in it, are granted with the others.
- **No defaults.** The cookie's name (`groundwork_session`) and the gap between two renewals of a session (an hour) were constants; they are the required settings `:cookie-name` and `:renew-after-minutes` of the `:sessions` part, doorman's shape. `find-session-key-of-request` takes the settings; `put-access-into-handlers`'s options are checked, every key required; `find-leaks` takes `{:seed … :size …}`, both required.
- `libraries.edn` no longer lists `:sessions` or `:access`; any server library may use doorman, through `doorman.api` only, which `libraries_test.clj` checks.

**Why one library, not two:** a request is let in at one door, where who is asking and what they may do are asked together. access already took the session step and read the person it put in the context, and sessions had no other user but the app's login: every change across the line between them would be two tags. This changes in part "A library says where it runs; sessions and access stay apart", which split them so each name said exactly one thing; the one name now says both.

**Why its own repository:** a `deps.edn` naming only cellar, mailroom, wire, malli, test.check and jsonista proves it needs nothing of Groundwork, and an app can take it alone. The name: the doorman knows who you are and whether you may go in; he stands at the house's door, beside the cellar, the backroom, the showroom and the mailroom.

**The price:** a change to doorman is a commit and a tag in another repository, then a bump here. An app's `:sessions` settings grow by two keys. Groundwork's tests no longer run doorman's; main's end-to-end test still signs in, asks, meets the gates and asks again after sync.

**Instead of:**
- two libraries, sessions and access, each its own repository: one door split in two, every change across them two tags
- staying folders in Groundwork's tree: no app can take them alone, and nothing proves their deps
- the names frontdesk and keyring: doorman was chosen over them
