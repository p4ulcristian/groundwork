# sessions v1: signed-in sessions, a step to find them

> Builds on [Sessions live in Postgres](../../../decisions/2026-10-12-sessions-live-in-postgres.md), [The session is the only truth](../../../decisions/2026-10-12-the-session-is-the-only-truth.md) and [Groundwork ships no logins](../../../decisions/2026-10-12-groundwork-ships-no-logins.md).

**Decision:**

- `sign-in!` makes a row for a person the app's login has proven and returns the Set-Cookie text: 32 random bytes, HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax, Secure; the table keeps only their SHA-256. `sign-out!` deletes the request's row; `sign-out-everywhere!` every row of a person; `delete-ended-sessions!` is for a nightly chore.
- `make-find-session-step` is the step at the start of a handler: a request that changes something (not a GET, or a websocket) must carry the app's own Origin, or it is answered with an error before any work; then `:groundwork.sessions/person-id` goes into the context, nil for nobody.
- A session lasts `:session-days`; each time it is seen its end moves out, at most once an hour.
- Its table is `sessions-table`, in the groundwork folder.

**Why:** the four things every request and every login need, and nothing an app must remember: the Origin check sits in the same step that reads the cookie.

**The price:** visitor sessions (a cart before signing in), embeddable pages and server-to-server routes (decided 2026-10-12) aren't in yet; visitors come next.

**Instead of:** the Origin check as a step of its own: one more to forget to put first.
